Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the country. Express shipping takes 2-3 business days and incurs an additional fee. Orders are typically processed and shipped within 24 hours of purchase, excluding weekends and holidays.

If a package is marked as delivered but the customer has not received it, the customer should first check with neighbors and building management. If the package still cannot be located after 48 hours, a replacement or refund may be issued at the discretion of support staff.

International shipping may take 10-20 business days depending on customs processing. The company is not responsible for customs fees or import duties charged by the destination country.
